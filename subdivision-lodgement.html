Subdivision Lodgement

Support with subdivision documentation and plan lodgement, including SPEAR lodgements where applicable.

Plans and approvals

Subdivision can create separate lots or change existing boundaries and interests. In Victoria, the process commonly involves a licensed land surveyor, a planning permit or other approval pathway, satisfaction of permit conditions and preparation of a plan for certification and registration. The steps depend on the site, the proposed layout and the relevant authority requirements. We can assist with the conveyancing documentation and registration-related steps within the agreed scope, and coordinate with the surveyor and other parties where needed.

SPEAR and title matters

SPEAR is used for relevant Victorian subdivision and plan processes. Where instructed and authorised, we can assist with the lodgement steps that fall within our role and monitor documentation needed to progress the plan. Existing mortgages, caveats, easements and other registered interests may affect registration or the resulting titles. The relevant lender or interest holder may need to provide consent or other documents. We help identify these conveyancing requirements and communicate with the surveyor, lender and relevant parties so that outstanding items can be addressed.

Registration and next steps

The timing of a subdivision depends on approvals, certification, authority requirements and the readiness of all necessary documents. We cannot guarantee a particular registration date or replace the work of a surveyor, planner or engineer. To discuss a matter, please provide the property address, current title or plan if available, the surveyor’s details, the approval or permit status and the intended subdivision outcome. We can then outline the conveyancing steps, information needed, likely coordination points and fees. Early contact is useful, especially where a sale, refinance or development timetable depends on new titles being created.
