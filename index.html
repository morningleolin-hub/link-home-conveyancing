ABOUT LINK HOME CONVEYANCING

Personal service, from a real person.

Link Home Conveyancing supports clients with property transactions across Victoria, including residential purchases and sales, ownership transfers, subdivision lodgements, development and off-the-plan matters, and commercial conveyancing.

We believe property matters deserve personal attention. You can speak directly with the person handling your conveyancing, receive updates as your matter progresses, and know that important details and deadlines are being followed up.

Many of our clients return to us for their next property transaction, and new clients often come through recommendations from people we have assisted. We value that trust and aim to provide careful, dependable support from the first enquiry through to settlement or registration.
