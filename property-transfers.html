Transfer of Property Ownership

Prepare transfer documents and coordinate registration for family, relationship, trust and other ownership changes.

Understand the proposed transfer

Discuss the reason for the transfer, current and proposed ownership, any mortgage and the documents needed. Transfers may arise from marriage or separation, estate arrangements, gifts, changes involving trusts or companies, or other circumstances.

Documents, duty and approvals

Prepare the relevant transfer documentation, which may include a Transfer of Land, nomination or supporting declarations. We identify duty and registration issues for consideration and coordinate information required for the State Revenue Office, lender or other relevant parties. Duty treatment depends on the facts and applicable rules.

Lodgement and registration

Arrange signing and verification requirements, coordinate with the lender where applicable, and lodge the documents through the appropriate electronic process. We follow up registration and advise when the title records have been updated. Tax, family law and structuring advice should be obtained from the appropriate adviser where needed.
