Commercial Conveyancing

Practical conveyancing support for commercial property purchases, sales, leases and related transactions.

Contract and title review

Review the contract, title and available property information for commercial, retail, office or industrial property. Depending on the transaction, we consider easements, planning information, leases, outgoings, GST clauses and special conditions, and identify issues for discussion with your other advisers.

Lease and transaction coordination

Assist with conveyancing aspects of lease assignments, transfers or related property arrangements. Liaise with agents, landlords, tenants, lenders and the other party’s representative to collect documents, track conditions and progress agreed requirements.

Settlement and registration

Prepare transfer and duty information, check adjustments and coordinate electronic settlement or registration where applicable. Commercial transactions can raise legal, tax and business issues beyond conveyancing; we can identify when separate specialist advice may be needed.
