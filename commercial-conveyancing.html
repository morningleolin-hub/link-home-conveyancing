Commercial Conveyancing

Conveyancing support for commercial property transactions and related arrangements.

Buying or selling commercial property

Commercial transactions may involve retail, office, industrial, warehouse or mixed-use property. The conveyancing review may cover title and plan details, easements, planning information, existing leases, owners corporation records, rates and outgoings, notices, mortgage arrangements and special conditions. We can assist with preparing or reviewing conveyancing documents, explaining key contract terms and coordinating the steps from contract to settlement. Commercial contracts may allocate responsibility for deposits, adjustments, risk and settlement differently from residential contracts, so the details should be checked against the proposed transaction.

Leases and occupancy

Where a property is leased, the lease and related records may be important to both parties. Relevant matters can include the lease term, options, rent reviews, permitted use, outgoings, security, incentives, make-good obligations and any known disputes or defaults. Vendors may need to provide lease information, while purchasers may need to understand the existing occupancy arrangements before proceeding. Retail Leases Act 2003 (Vic) requirements may be relevant to some premises. We can assist with agreed conveyancing and lease-related documentation; complex negotiations or disputes may require a separately agreed legal scope.

Coordinating the transaction

Commercial matters may also involve lender requirements, mortgage discharges, caveats, guarantees, settlement adjustments and multiple parties. We coordinate with the other conveyancer, agent, lender and relevant professionals, prepare transfer and duty information and arrange electronic settlement where applicable. Questions about GST, duty, tax or financial outcomes may require advice from an appropriately qualified specialist; we do not assume a particular treatment without the necessary information. To start, provide the property address, whether you are buying or selling, the proposed contract, lease details, lender involvement and expected settlement date. We can discuss the conveyancing work, required documents and fees.
