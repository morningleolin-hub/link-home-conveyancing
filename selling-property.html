Selling Property

Prepare the sale documents, respond to enquiries and coordinate settlement and payment of sale proceeds.

Contract and Section 32 preparation

Prepare the Contract of Sale and Section 32 Vendor Statement using the information and certificates relevant to your property. We review title details, rates and other available records, and identify information needed from you or third parties. Additional searches or specialist advice may be required depending on the property.

Enquiries and contract changes

Liaise with your estate agent and the purchaser’s conveyancer, respond to conveyancing enquiries and help document agreed contract changes. We keep track of the settlement date and follow up outstanding documents or requirements.

Settlement and proceeds

Prepare for electronic settlement, check adjustments and coordinate with your lender if a mortgage is to be discharged. We confirm the settlement figures and payment directions with you and follow up completion of the transaction.
