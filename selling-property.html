Selling Property

Practical support for vendor statements, contracts and settlement.

Contract and Section 32 documents

Before a property is marketed, the vendor generally needs a Contract of Sale and a Section 32 Vendor Statement that meets Victorian requirements. We help prepare or review these documents and identify information that may be required, such as title and plan details, mortgages, easements, planning information, rates and charges, owners corporation records and relevant notices. The documents must accurately disclose the required information, so please tell us about known issues and provide any available records. Early preparation can help identify missing information before the sale campaign becomes urgent.

Agreeing the sale terms

We help ensure the contract reflects the agreed price, deposit, settlement date, inclusions, exclusions and any special conditions. Private sales and auctions can involve different practical considerations. An auction contract is generally binding once signed after the successful bid, so the documents and arrangements should be checked before auction day. For a private sale, any finance, building, pest or other conditions should be clearly recorded. Let us know if the property is tenanted, part of an owners corporation, vacant land or affected by building works, subdivision or other title matters.

From contract to settlement

Once the contract is signed, we monitor key dates and coordinate the conveyancing steps. These may include obtaining mortgage payout figures, arranging mortgage discharge, responding to conveyancing enquiries, calculating settlement adjustments and preparing transfer and duty information. We liaise with the purchaser’s conveyancer, agent and lender to confirm the documents and figures needed for settlement. Please provide the property address, agent details, mortgage information and anticipated sale dates as early as possible. We will explain the required steps, discuss any issues that arise and confirm the scope and fees before commencing.
