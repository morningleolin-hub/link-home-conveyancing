ABOUT LINK HOME CONVEYANCING

Trusted relationships. Thoughtful service. Attention to detail.

Link Home Conveyancing provides conveyancing services across Victoria for residential buyers and sellers, families arranging ownership transfers, property owners undertaking subdivisions, developers and commercial property clients.

We value the relationships built with our clients over time. Many clients return to us when they move, invest or undertake another property transaction, and a significant number of new matters come through recommendations from previous clients, family members, friends and professional contacts. We regard that continued trust as an important reflection of the care and consistency we aim to bring to every matter.

Each transaction has its own documents, timeframes and practical considerations. We take a careful, organised approach to reviewing the relevant conveyancing documents, coordinating with the other parties and keeping track of key steps from the initial instructions through to settlement or registration. We aim to provide responsive service, explain what information is needed and keep clients informed about the progress of their matter.

Whether you are buying your first property, selling an established home, transferring an interest in property, arranging a subdivision or dealing with a commercial transaction, our focus is on dependable conveyancing support tailored to the requirements of the matter.

We appreciate the confidence clients place in us and understand that property transactions often involve important personal and financial decisions. We approach each matter with professionalism, discretion and attention to detail.
