About Link Home Conveyancing

Personal service, with direct involvement from start to finish.

You deal directly with the person handling your matter

At Link Home Conveyancing, your matter is handled personally by an experienced conveyancing professional. We take the time to understand what you are doing, explain the documents and important dates, follow up outstanding items and keep you informed as the transaction progresses. You can contact us with questions and speak with the person who knows your file.

Relationships built over time

Many clients return to us when they buy, sell or transfer property again. We are also grateful when clients recommend us to family, friends and colleagues. Those ongoing relationships matter to us, and we aim to provide the same careful, practical service each time.

Practical work, carefully followed through

Our work covers residential purchases and sales, property ownership transfers, subdivision lodgements, development and off-the-plan matters, and commercial conveyancing across Victoria. We review relevant documents, explain conveyancing requirements, coordinate with the other parties and follow through on the steps needed for settlement or registration. Where a matter calls for specialist legal, tax, planning or building advice outside conveyancing, we will explain that distinction.
