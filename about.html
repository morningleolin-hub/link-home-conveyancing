ABOUT LINK HOME CONVEYANCING

Personal service. Real people. Trusted relationships.

Link Home Conveyancing provides conveyancing services across Victoria for residential buyers and sellers, families arranging property transfers, subdivision matters, developers and commercial property clients.

Our service is delivered by real people who take time to understand each client’s circumstances and respond to the practical details of their matter. Property transactions can be stressful and time-sensitive, and we believe clients should be able to speak with a person who knows their file, understands the next steps and can follow up when something needs attention.

Many clients return to us for later purchases, sales or other property matters. We also receive referrals from past clients, family members, friends and professional contacts. We value this trust and see it as a meaningful part of our work: relationships are built through consistent service, careful handling of documents and taking responsibility for the details that matter.
