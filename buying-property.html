Buying Property

Professional advice and support from contract to settlement.

Contract and Section 32 review

Review the Contract of Sale and Section 32 Vendor Statement before you sign. We check title details, easements, covenants, planning information, owners corporation records where relevant, deposit terms, settlement date and special conditions. We explain conveyancing risks and identify questions to raise with the vendor or agent. For auction purchases, arrange the review before bidding.

Key dates and documents

After signing, diarise contractual dates and follow up requirements for finance, inspections and other conditions where applicable. We prepare and check transfer and duty information, request relevant certificates and communicate with the other conveyancer about outstanding items.

Settlement coordination

Liaise with your lender, the vendor’s conveyancer and other parties to prepare for electronic settlement through PEXA. We check settlement adjustments, confirm the figures and documents needed from you, and keep you informed of progress and any outstanding steps.
