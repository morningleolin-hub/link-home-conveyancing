Buying Property

Professional conveyancing support to help you buy property in Victoria with confidence.

Before you sign

We can review the Contract of Sale and Section 32 Vendor Statement before you commit to a purchase. Depending on the property, we check matters such as title details, easements, covenants, planning information, owners corporation records, rates and relevant contract conditions. We explain the conveyancing implications of key clauses, including the deposit, settlement date and any finance or inspection conditions, and identify questions that may need to be raised with the vendor or agent. If you are buying at auction, the contract is generally binding when your bid is accepted, so arranging a review beforehand is important. A conveyancing review does not replace building, pest, planning or other specialist inspections.

Preparing for settlement

After the contract is signed, we track the important dates and coordinate the steps required for settlement. This may include liaising with the vendor’s conveyancer, agent and lender; preparing transfer and duty information; checking settlement adjustments; and arranging electronic settlement. We will let you know what documents, signatures and funds are needed and when they are due. If you are buying with another person, through a company or trustee, or under a particular ownership arrangement, please raise this before signing so the purchaser details can be considered early.

A practical, organised process

Every property is different. We work through the documents relevant to your transaction, explain issues that affect the conveyancing process and flag matters that may need input from a planner, building consultant, lender or other professional. Finance approval and the release of funds remain the responsibility of the lender and borrower, and no settlement date can be guaranteed where other parties or approvals are involved. Contact us with the property address, proposed purchase details and any draft contract available. We can discuss the work required, the information needed and our fees before proceeding.
