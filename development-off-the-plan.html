Property Development & Off-the-Plan

Conveyancing support for development projects, off-the-plan sales and related documentation.

Developer-side preparation

Assist with conveyancing documents for residential and mixed-use developments, including off-the-plan sales and townhouse or unit projects. Coordinate with the developer, surveyor and other advisers on contract documentation, proposed plans and relevant disclosure material.

Purchaser contract review

Review the Contract of Sale and disclosure documents, explain key conditions, proposed plan details, sunset dates and other important contractual dates. We identify conveyancing issues to raise with the developer or your other advisers; planning, building, finance and tax advice may need separate specialists.

Plan registration and settlement

Track contractual milestones and coordinate with relevant parties as the plan progresses toward registration. For purchasers, we liaise with the developer’s conveyancer and lender and prepare for settlement once the required conditions and registration steps are complete.
