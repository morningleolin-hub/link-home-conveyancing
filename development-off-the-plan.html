Property Development & Off-the-Plan

Conveyancing support for development projects, off-the-plan sales and related documentation.

Developer documentation

Development and off-the-plan transactions require coordination between land, plan and contract details. For developers selling new lots or dwellings, we can assist with conveyancing documentation, contract preparation or review within the agreed scope, and coordination of relevant title and plan information. It is important that lot descriptions, proposed plans, deposit arrangements, settlement triggers and special conditions are consistent with the transaction. Where a project includes multiple lots or staged sales, early document coordination can help reduce avoidable administrative problems.

Purchasing off the plan

An off-the-plan purchaser may sign a contract before the final title exists or before construction is complete. The contract may address proposed plans, variations, sunset dates, deposit arrangements and the events that trigger settlement. We can review the conveyancing documents, explain key contractual steps and track dates and requirements. Purchasers should consider the contract carefully before signing and may need separate building, valuation, finance or other specialist advice. We can help identify conveyancing questions to raise before commitment, but cannot guarantee construction progress, valuation, finance approval or a particular registration date.

Milestones and settlement

Development timelines can depend on planning or certification decisions, construction progress, plan registration, lender requirements and completion documents. We help coordinate the conveyancing steps, communicate with the other parties and identify outstanding information as milestones approach. When titles are ready, the process may involve registration, duty information, lender consents, adjustments and electronic settlement. To discuss a project, provide the location, whether you act for a developer or purchaser, the draft contract, plan or title status, expected milestones and number of lots. We can then confirm the proposed scope, information required and fees.
